Systems & Microarchitectural Security Researcher

Amit Choudhari

I am a PhD student and research assistant at CISPA — Helmholtz Center for Information Security, advised by Prof. Christian Rossow. My research spans microarchitectural security, systems security, network security, trusted execution, and hardware–software security.

Before my PhD, I worked on secure and embedded systems in industry, including ARM TrustZone, hypervisors, secure I/O paths, Linux, firmware, and embedded platforms.

Research

I study security at the boundary between hardware and software: how low-level system behavior creates security problems, how those behaviors can be measured and understood, and how systems can be designed to reduce the resulting attack surface.
