Systems & Microarchitectural Security Researcher

Amit Choudhari

PhD Student · CISPA Helmholtz Center for Information Security

I am a PhD student at CISPA, advised by Prof. Christian Rossow, working on microarchitectural and systems security. My recent research covers secure in-memory computing, malicious SmartNICs, and reverse-engineering undocumented CPU prefetchers.

Before my PhD, I worked on trusted execution and embedded systems, including ARM TrustZone and hypervisor-based security at Qualcomm.

Open to research & R&D opportunities in systems and hardware security.

Memclave

ESORICS 2025

NICraft

Shows that malicious NIC firmware can exploit Linux IP reassembly to mount fine-grained host cache side channels, including keystroke-activity detection at over 96% precision.

ACM CCS 2023

FetchBench

A cross-platform framework that characterized undocumented prefetchers on 19 ARM and x86-64 CPUs and uncovered a previously unknown replay-based prefetcher on Cortex-A72.
